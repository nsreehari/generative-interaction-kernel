# Contributor Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community
for everyone who contributes to or participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome people of all backgrounds, identities, experiences, and skill levels.
- Give and accept feedback with empathy, and focus discussion on ideas and the
  project.
- Respect differing viewpoints, boundaries, and privacy.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and learn from
  the experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is
representing the project or its community, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and project-related events.

## Reporting and enforcement

For abuse that violates GitHub's policies, use GitHub's built-in reporting and
blocking tools. For other conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through
the contact options on the [owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/nsreehari).

Repository owners will review reports fairly and respect the privacy and safety
of those involved as far as possible. Responses will be proportionate to the
severity, impact, and history of the behavior and may include a private
correction, a warning, temporary limits on participation, or permanent removal
from project spaces. Immediate action may be taken when needed to protect the
community.
